Touch and volume

  1. Lesson 11R legato – L staccato

    Same notes and rhythm, different note lengths.

    Listen for: R connects the notes, L releases them short.

    Pass when: Hear the two touches clearly apart.

    Open in the app →

  2. Lesson 12R staccato – L legato

    Swap the touches.

    Listen for: L stays legato even though R plays short.

    Pass when: Don't let R pull L into playing short.

    Open in the app →

  3. Lesson 13R forward – L background

    Same rhythm, layered volume.

    Listen for: R melody clear; L like a whisper.

    Pass when: Hear R stand out in all 4 bars.

    Open in the app →

  4. Lesson 14Switch the sound layers

    First two bars R stands out; last two bars L stands out.

    Listen for: Change the dynamics right at the start of bar 3.

    Pass when: Don't change tempo when swapping roles.

    Open in the app →

  5. Lesson 15Offset accents

    R accents beat 1; L accents beat 3.

    Listen for: Accent with arm weight, without tensing.

    Pass when: Hear two separate accents.

    Open in the app →
