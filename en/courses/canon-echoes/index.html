Canon echoes

  1. Lesson 31One-bar echo

    Play one phrase in both hands with different entry times.

    Listen for: Delay: 4 beats; the leading hand carries on as the other begins.

    Pass when: The left hand enters exactly one bar later; the right hand doesn't stop.

  2. Lesson 32Half-bar echo

    Play one phrase in both hands with different entry times.

    Listen for: Delay: 2 beats; the leading hand carries on as the other begins.

    Pass when: The left hand enters two beats later, keeping the phrase that's playing.

    Pass when: The left hand leads the phrase; the right hand enters exactly one bar later.
