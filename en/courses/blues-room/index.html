Blues and swing

  1. Lesson 36

    Shuffle engine

    The left hand keeps the long–short rhythm; the right hand keeps quarter notes.

    Listen for: Hear the long–short pair in every beat.

    Pass when: The left hand keeps the swing; the right hand doesn't shift its quarter notes.

    Open →
  2. Lesson 37

    Swing over a straight beat

    The right hand swings over the left hand's four even beats.

    Listen for: The short note at the end of each beat; don't split the beat in half.

    Pass when: The left hand stays even while the right hand plays long–short.

    Open →
  3. Lesson 38

    Chords at the end of the beat

    Left hand shuffles; right hand places chords on the short part at the end of the beat.

    Listen for: The right hand rests on the start of the beat and waits for the short part.

    Pass when: Don't play the right-hand chord early with the left-hand bass.

    Open →
  4. Lesson 39

    A blues phrase over a loop

    Play a swung blues phrase over the shuffle accompaniment.

    Listen for: Hear E-flat and B-flat clearly; the accompaniment stays light.

    Pass when: Don't stop the bass when the right hand changes to a flat.

    Open →
  5. Lesson 40

    Little blues stage

    Keep the shuffle under a blues phrase with rests.

    Listen for: The left hand carries on while the right hand rests a full beat.

    Pass when: Three runs without filling the rests, the bass swinging evenly.
