Independent motion

  1. Lesson 21One-octave parallel scale

    Hands in the same direction; watch the thumb-under.

    Listen for: Pause briefly at the thumb-under, then reconnect.

    Pass when: Don't twist the wrist crossing E-F.

    Open in the app →

  2. Lesson 22Eight notes in contrary motion

    R goes up while L goes down.

    Listen for: R ascends from C4, L descends from C3; each hand changes direction independently.

    Pass when: The hands meet exactly at the bar line.

    Open in the app →

  3. Lesson 23Inverted chords + bass

    R changes chord position; L walks the bass.

    Listen for: R changes chord shape exactly on beat 3.

    Pass when: L notes stay even while R changes position.

    Open in the app →

  4. Lesson 24Alberti accompaniment

    L low–high–middle–high; R plays the melody.

    Listen for: Keep L even like a turning wheel, without tension.

    Pass when: R still stands out over L.

    Open in the app →

  5. Lesson 25Walking bass + off-beat chords

    L walks on each beat; R plays chords on the “and”.

    Listen for: R doesn't come in early with the bass.

    Pass when: 8 accurate off-beat placements in a row.
