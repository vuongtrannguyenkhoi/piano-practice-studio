Melody and chords

  1. Lesson 16Melody + block chords

    Each bar L holds one chord; R plays the melody.

    Listen for: L plays the chord all at once on beat 1.

    Pass when: R doesn't stop when the chord changes.

    Open in the app →

  2. Lesson 17Melody + root/fifth

    L plays the root and the fifth.

    Listen for: L keeps four even beats.

    Pass when: Change the chord root exactly on each bar.

    Open in the app →

  3. Lesson 18Melody + broken 1-5-3-5

    L arpeggiates the chord in eighth notes.

    Listen for: L doesn't speed up on the broken notes.

    Pass when: Eight even L notes while R can still sing.

    Open in the app →

  4. Lesson 19Waltz: bass – chord – chord

    3/4 time, L accompanies in waltz style.

    Listen for: A light accent on beat 1, beats 2-3 soft.

    Pass when: Don't let it turn into 4/4.

    Open in the app →

  5. Lesson 20Swap the melody role

    L plays a low melody, R holds the chords.

    Listen for: Hear the melody in L; R soft.

    Pass when: L stands out over R for 4 bars.
