Advanced applications

  1. Lesson 26Three against two

    In one 3/4 bar: R plays 3, L plays 2.

    Listen for: Divide the bar into 6 parts: R enters on 1–3–5, L on 1–4; these are counts, not finger numbers.

    Pass when: Separate 3:2 cleanly without speeding up.

    Open in the app →

  2. Lesson 27Syncopation with chord changes

    R eighth notes with rests; L changes chords on beats 1 and 3.

    Listen for: R keeps the rests, L changes chords on time.

    Pass when: Don't fill the rests with extra notes.

    Open in the app →

  3. Lesson 28Call and response between the hands

    L repeats R's motif one beat later.

    Listen for: Sing each voice separately, then combine.

    Pass when: The hands never sound together where a rest is written.

    Open in the app →

  4. Lesson 29Waltz with two separate lines

    3/4 time: R eighth-note melody, L bass–chord.

    Listen for: L is the dance beat; R is the song.

    Pass when: A full 3 beats per bar; R doesn't crowd L.

    Open in the app →

  5. Lesson 30Final etude: keep three things going

    R syncopated melody; L broken-chord accompaniment; changing dynamics.

    Listen for: R forward; L even; every rest exact.

    Pass when: 3 complete runs at 70 BPM without stopping.

    Open in the app →
